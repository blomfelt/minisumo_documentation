To make sure the robot can distinguish between the light and dark areas of the arena the IR sensors needs to be calibrated by turning the small screw on top of the blue box of the sensor. 

When the robot is placed on the edge of the arena, turn the screw until the light is turned on when the sensor is located over the white part of the arena and off when over the black area. Do the same procedure for both sensors.
